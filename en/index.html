GOAT: film one swing, and AI tells you what to work on

Film your golf swing with your phone. GOAT tracks your body on the phone, finds the key moments of the swing, and an AI coach tells you the one or two things to fix first.

About us

Rode Sports Technology Co., Ltd. is a sports technology company based in Hsinchu, Taiwan, founded in 2026. We apply sports science and artificial intelligence to help people get better at the sports they love. Our first product is GOAT (Golf of AI Time), a golf swing analysis and coaching app for iPhone and Android.

Most golfers practise alone and cannot see what their own swing looks like. We built GOAT so that anyone who practises can find out, within about twenty seconds, which part of the swing needs work and what to do about it.

Our product: GOAT

GOAT is a phone app for golfers of every level, from complete beginners to experienced players. It is designed in Traditional Chinese for golfers in Taiwan.

Film your swing

Record a swing in the app, or choose a video from your photo library. Face-on and down-the-line views are both supported, for right-handed and left-handed golfers, with woods and irons. A short guide shows how to place the phone.

On-device body tracking

The app follows your joints through the swing on the phone itself and picks out four key moments: address, top of the backswing, impact and finish. Your video is never uploaded.

Score and measurements

Each swing gets a score and a few simple measurements, with the tracked skeleton drawn over the video. From the front: head steadiness and whether your hips moved toward the front foot. From the side: backswing turn, keeping your forward lean and hip turn.

AI coaching advice

With your consent, four still frames of the swing go to an AI coach, which answers with a short three-part note suited to your level, your handedness and the club you used: what you did well, the one thing to fix, and one action for your next ball.

Daily lessons

Short daily lessons on fundamentals, etiquette, the rules of golf and technique. Each lesson is a few cards and a quick quiz, laid out as an 18-hole course you play through. Technique lessons follow what your own swing analysis found.

History and progress

Past analyses are kept so you can look back and see which measurements have improved since your last swing from the same angle.

How it works

  1. Film. Set the phone at about waist height, with your whole body and the club in the picture, and make one swing.
  2. Analyse. In about 20 seconds the app tracks your body, finds the key moments and scores the swing. This happens on your phone.
  3. Get advice. The AI coach answers with a short three-part note: what you did well, the one thing to fix, and one action for your next ball.
  4. Learn. Take the day's lesson, and come back tomorrow to see your progress.

Pricing

GOAT is free to download and use. Body tracking and scores are unlimited on the free plan. An optional auto-renewing subscription, sold through the App Store or Google Play, adds more AI coaching and lessons: there are two paid plans, Plus and Pro. Prices are shown in the app and in the store. The app shows no advertising.

Privacy

Your videos stay on your phone. Only four still frames and the tracking data are uploaded, and only when you are signed in. We do not sell data, show advertising, or track you across other apps. Read the full Privacy Policy.
