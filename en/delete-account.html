Delete your GOAT account

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This is a translation of the Traditional Chinese page; if the two differ, the Chinese version applies.

You can delete your account with GOAT (Rode Sports Technology Co., Ltd.) at any time, without giving a reason. There are two ways.

1. In the app (fastest)

  1. Open GOAT and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap 設定 (Settings) at the bottom.
  3. Tap 刪除帳號 (Delete account) and confirm.

The account is deleted at once and cannot be restored.

2. Without the app: ask us by email

Write to goatappsupport@gmail.com from the email address of your GOAT account, with the subject "Delete my GOAT account".

  • If you signed in with Google or Apple, write from that account's email address.
  • If you signed in with Apple and chose "Hide My Email": deleting in the app is fastest. If you cannot use the app, write to us and include the relay address Apple created for you (it ends in @privaterelay.appleid.com; on your iPhone you can find it under Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → GOAT).
  • We reply once to confirm, delete the account within 30 days of your confirmation, and tell you when it is done.
  • If the sender's address is not the account's address we cannot confirm it is you, and will ask you to write from the account's address.

What is deleted

  • The account (email, name, sign-in data)
  • Every analysis: scores, coaching text, and the stills and skeleton data stored in the cloud
  • The golfer questionnaire, lesson progress, usage records and the record of your AI data-sharing choice
  • Feedback reports you sent (text, voice notes, screenshots)
  • Your customer record at our subscription provider (RevenueCat)

What is kept

  • De-identified AI usage statistics (how many AI requests were made on a day, and what they cost). They contain no account ID, email, still or text and cannot be linked back to you. We keep them to control costs.
  • Purchase records at Apple or Google Play are held by them, not by us; we cannot delete them.
  • What is on your phone (the original videos, the app's local data) is not on our servers. Deleting the app removes its data; the videos in your photo library are yours to keep or delete.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription

If you have a paid subscription, cancel it first, otherwise the store keeps charging you.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name at the top → Subscriptions → GOAT → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → GOAT → Cancel subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google Play; we cannot refund you directly.

Deleting only some data

You do not have to delete the account: in the app's 紀錄 (Records) tab you can delete a single analysis (its stills and data go with it), and under 設定 → AI 資料分享 you can withdraw AI data sharing. For anything else, write to us. Details are in the privacy policy.
