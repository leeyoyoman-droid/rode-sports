GOAT Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This is a translation of the Traditional Chinese policy; if the two differ, the Chinese version applies.

GOAT is a golf swing analysis app made by Rode Sports Technology Co., Ltd. The videos you film stay on your phone and are not uploaded. After you sign in, the four still frames and the body-tracking data used for the analysis are uploaded. When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it also sends a crash report that does not include your account. We do not sell data, we do not show advertising, and we do not track what you do in other apps.

What we do not collect:

  • Your original video: it exists only on your phone. The analysis is done on the phone and the video is not uploaded.
  • Analyses made without signing in: you can analyse a video and see the score without an account. In that case the stills and the data stay on the phone; they are not uploaded and not sent to the AI.
  • Payment details: card and other payment details are handled by Apple (iPhone app) or Google Play (Android app). We never receive them.
  • Location, contacts, or other photos in your library: we do not read them. A video you choose from your photo library is used only for the analysis.
  • Store ratings: when the app asks you to rate it, it uses the rating sheet of Apple or Google Play. We cannot see whether you rated the app or what rating you gave.

Microphone permission: filming a swing does not record sound. A voice note is recorded and sent to our support only when you hold the record button in a feedback report.

Camera permission: the app needs the camera to film your swing. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's Settings; you can still analyse videos chosen from your photo library.

Notification permission: the daily lesson reminder is a local notification scheduled by the phone itself. It does not go through our servers, we do not collect a push token, and no data leaves your phone. The app asks for notification permission only after you finish your first lesson. The reminder is set to 8 pm by default; you can turn it off or change the time in 設定 (Settings). It stops by itself after 7 days without opening the app.

How we use the data

We use your data only to provide GOAT:

  • To produce AI coaching advice: after you consent, the four stills, the camera angle, the type of club, your handedness, score range and years of play, and the state of the body-movement measurements that the result screen shows for that video (up to two for a face-on video and up to three for a down-the-line video, as plain words such as "steady", "just right" or "low", never numbers; a measurement that could not be taken reliably or is not shown is not sent) are sent through our server to Google's Gemini AI, which writes advice suited to your level.
  • To keep your history: so you can look back at earlier analyses and see your progress.
  • To arrange lessons: the next lesson is recommended from your analysis results and your lesson progress.
  • To manage paid features: to check whether you are a paying member and to count each plan's uses.
  • To handle your reports: to read the problem you reported, answer you and fix the app. Voice notes and screenshots are seen only by our support and development staff; they are not sent to the AI and not used for anything else.
  • To maintain and improve the service: to find errors, control the cost of the AI service and improve the accuracy of the analysis. If we ever want to use your data to train our own analysis models, we will ask for your consent first.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track what you do in other apps or websites.

Consent to AI data sharing: before your first analysis the app explains which data goes to Google Gemini, and you decide whether to agree. You can withdraw consent at any time in 設定 (Settings) → AI 資料分享 (AI Data Sharing). The AI coach is then paused; body tracking and scores keep working.

Deleting your account: you can delete it at any time in the app under 設定 (Settings) → 刪除帳號 (Delete Account). Your account, analysis history, stills in the cloud, questionnaire, lesson progress and usage records are then deleted permanently, together with your customer record at RevenueCat. This cannot be undone. De-identified statistics of AI usage are kept to control costs. Purchase records kept by Apple and Google Play are not held by us, and we cannot delete them. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke GOAT's sign-in access when you delete your account, so GOAT no longer appears in your iPhone's list of apps using Sign in with Apple. If Apple cannot be reached at that moment, GOAT may stay in that list. You can remove it yourself in iPhone Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple, then choose GOAT and tap Stop Using Sign in with Apple.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: if you are a paying member, cancel the subscription first, otherwise Apple or Google Play keeps charging you. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: open Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → GOAT → Cancel subscription.

You can also ask us to delete your account without installing the app: see How to delete your account.

You can also delete single analyses in your history; their stills and data are deleted with them.

Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act you may, for your own data: ask what we hold or view it, request a copy, supplement or correct it, ask us to stop collecting, processing or using it, and ask for it to be deleted. Most of this can be done directly in the app (edit the questionnaire, withdraw AI data-sharing consent, delete records, delete your account). For anything else, write to us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Please do not use GOAT if you are under 13. If you are under 18, please use it only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you find that we have received a child's data without consent, please tell us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when features or the law change. For important changes (for example collecting a new type of data) we will notify you in the app. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
