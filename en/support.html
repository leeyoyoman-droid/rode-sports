Filming and analysis

How should I film for the most accurate analysis?

Place the phone at about waist height and film either face-on (the camera facing you) or down the line (from behind, looking towards the target). Your whole body and the club must be in the picture. The app shows a filming guide the first time you use it.

How long does an analysis take? Can I switch to another app?

About 20 seconds. Please stay on the GOAT screen while it runs. If you leave, the phone pauses the analysis, and the app starts it again by itself when you come back.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Body tracking is done on your phone. When you are signed in, four still frames of the key moments and the tracking data are uploaded to keep your history. The AI coach sees only those four stills, the camera angle, the club, your questionnaire answers and the state of the measurements shown on the result screen as plain words (such as "steady" or "just right"), not the tracking numbers. See the Privacy Policy.

I play left-handed. Can I use it?

Yes. Choose "left-handed" in the questionnaire or in 設定 (Settings), and the analysis reads the swing in the left-handed direction.

Subscription and payment

What is the difference between the Free, Plus and Pro plans?

All three plans have unlimited body tracking and scores. Free: AI coaching once a day, one lesson a day, and the stills of your 3 most recent analyses are kept. Plus: AI coaching 3 times a day, unlimited lessons, and the stills of the 30 most recent analyses are kept. Pro: unlimited AI coaching (fair use), unlimited lessons, and the stills of the 100 most recent analyses are kept. Fair use is explained in section 3 of the Terms of Use.

Is there a free trial?

New subscribers get a 7-day free trial on a first subscription (one trial per store account). If you do not cancel before it ends, you are charged the price of the plan you chose.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by the store you bought from. iPhone: go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GOAT and cancel there. Android: open Google Play → your profile picture at the top right → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → GOAT → Cancel subscription. If you cancel before the end of the current period you are not charged again.

I changed phones or reinstalled the app and my membership is gone.

In the app go to 設定 (Settings) → 恢復購買 (Restore Purchases). Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you bought with and the membership comes back.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by the store; we cannot refund you directly. If you bought through Apple, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you bought through Google Play, request one from your Google Play order history, or see Google Play's refund help.

Account

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to 設定 (Settings) → 刪除帳號 (Delete Account). Your account, analysis history, cloud stills, questionnaire, lesson progress and usage records are then deleted permanently. De-identified AI usage statistics are kept. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple or Google Play subscription: if you are a paying member, cancel the subscription first.

What if I do not want my data sent to the AI?

Withdraw your consent in 設定 (Settings) → AI 資料分享 (AI Data Sharing). The AI coach is then paused; body tracking and scores keep working as before.
