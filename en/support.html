Filming and analysis

How should I film for the most accurate analysis?

Place the phone at about waist height and film either face-on (the camera facing you) or down the line (from behind, looking towards the target). Your whole body and the club must be in the picture. The app shows a filming guide the first time you use it.

How long does an analysis take? Can I switch to another app?

About 20 seconds. Please stay on the GOAT screen while it runs. If you leave, iOS pauses the analysis, and the app starts it again by itself when you come back.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Body tracking is done on your phone. Only four still frames of the key moments and the tracking data are uploaded, to produce the AI coaching advice and to keep your history. See the Privacy Policy.

I play left-handed. Can I use it?

Yes. Choose "left-handed" in the questionnaire or in Settings, and the analysis reads the swing in the left-handed direction.

Subscription and payment

What is the difference between the free and the paid version?

Free: unlimited body tracking and scores, AI coaching once a day, one lesson a day, and the stills of your 3 most recent analyses are kept. Paid (GOAT Pro): unlimited AI coaching and lessons, a comparison picture against a reference swing, and the stills of 30 analyses are kept.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GOAT and cancel there. If you cancel before the end of the current period you are not charged again.

I changed phones or reinstalled the app and my membership is gone.

In the app go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID and the membership comes back.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Settings → Delete Account. Your account, your analysis history and the stills stored in the cloud are then deleted permanently. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription: if you are a paying member, cancel the subscription first.

What if I do not want my data sent to the AI?

Withdraw your consent in Settings → AI Data Sharing. The AI coach is then paused; body tracking and scores keep working as before.
